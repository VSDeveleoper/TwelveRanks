# Twelve Ranks Community Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Twelve Ranks aims to provide a respectful, constructive, and accessible place
for people to collaborate. Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and review work based on evidence and project requirements;
- communicate respectfully across differences in experience, language,
  culture, identity, and opinion;
- give specific, actionable feedback without personal attacks;
- respect privacy, attribution, intellectual-property rights, and contributor
  boundaries; and
- accept maintainer decisions while remaining free to fork the Apache-2.0
  project and pursue a different direction lawfully.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, stalking, doxxing,
impersonation, deliberate intimidation, repeated unwanted contact, spam, and
publishing another person's private information are not permitted.

Do not pressure contributors into unpaid work, undisclosed commercial terms,
license changes, exclusivity, or the surrender of rights beyond the terms they
knowingly accepted.

## Scope

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
community spaces, and public interactions where someone is representing Twelve
Ranks or acting on behalf of the project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Use GitHub's private content-reporting and abuse-reporting controls when they
are available. If the relevant page does not provide a report control, use
[GitHub Support's report-abuse form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Do not publish sensitive conduct details in an issue.

Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the
conduct-reporting process.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, request corrective action, limit
participation, lock a conversation, reject a contribution, or ban a participant
when reasonably necessary to protect the community and project. Enforcement
will consider context, severity, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of
affected people.

Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is prohibited.
